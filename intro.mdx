## Content Overview

To talk about leadership, we must talk about a lot of things, one by one.

### **Direction**

- Leadership starts with a [direction](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/direction-XwCn7Zx7.html). First, we must know where we are going.
- Before going anywhere, we must know why. We must have a [purpose](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/purpose-pqEhxCLq.html).
- Then, we must know what we will find there. We need a [vision](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/vision-o2QwCyiE.html).
- But to actually get there, we need some sort of [strategy](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/strategy-8eTXA3kS.html), otherwise we will get lost.
- Finally, we are ready to begin our journey.

### **Trust**

- We need to find companions whom we can fully [trust](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/trust-6kG0MOYN.html) and rely on.
- Trust is the most fundamental building block of leadership.
- To talk about trust, we must know what [types of trust](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/types-of-trust-vj2K3mU6.html) there are.
- And we must also know that trust evolves over time, so we have [stages of trust](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/stages-of-trust-VVQhiEK6.html).
- But trust is a complicated issue, and it depends on our [states of mind](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/states-of-mind-p5KXrGTp.html).
- It also depends on our feeling of safety, so we need to know the [types of safety](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/types-of-safety-Am2tbrMd.html).
- In order to create safety, we need to set different [types of boundaries](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/types-of-boundaries-jO9ipYXj.html).
- And trust can be easily broken if we are not careful due to our [cognitive biases](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/cognitive-biases-z8CHTXxT.html).

### **Communication**

- Trust is very much based on our interaction and communication.
- Communication is a complex topic that includes multiple aspects.
- We must understand the different [styles of communication](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/communication-styles-fr8Fx6Cr.html) that people use.
- We must also understand what [types of feedback](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/types-of-feedback-cOLxKXQn.html) we can give and receive.
- And also, what [styles of appreciation](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/appreciation-styles-zdHfQpUM.html) and [apology](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/apology-styles-mVPWwbHo.html) people prefer.

### **Coordination**

- Once we can manage trust and communication, we can start moving together.
- To coordinate with each other, we must understand our [leadership style](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/leadership-styles-NDI1PWik.html).
- And we must consider which [followership styles](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/followership-styles-req0e7HQ-req0e7HQ.html) align with our leadership.
- We should also identify which [sources of power](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/sources-of-power-bXxId2Xm.html) are available to us.
- And most likely, we will need help from one or more [supportive roles](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/supportive-roles-m5hjKbQn.html).
- Leadership is all about decisions, so we need a [decision-making process](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/process-7UZRL0TU.html).
- We also need to acknowledge different [decision-making styles](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/styles-Vr1X9gll-Vr1X9gll.html).
- And we need to ensure that we make the necessary [types of decisions](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/types-of-decisions-fIMYR051-fIMYR051.html).

### **Culture**

- When there is coordination and communication, there is a group [culture](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/culture-ygIzJARC.html).

### **Diversity**

- But to talk about culture, we must inevitably talk about [diversity](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/diversity-0lx6vmgm.html).

### **Maturity**

- If we want to grow, we must develop and increase our [maturity](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/maturity-K5iTpK04-K5iTpK04.html).

### **Change**

- We should always be ready for [change](https://morco.ro/leadership-system/html/change-nFs4aQLq.html), in both ourselves and our company.
