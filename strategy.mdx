<Columns cols={2}>
  <Column>
    <Card type="info">
      **Strategy** = the way in which we orchestrate our goals, plans, priorities, actions, and resources in order to achieve our desired final outcome. It outlines how the company intends to create value, compete in its industry, achieve growth, and sustain profitability over time.
    </Card>

    <Card title="Our strategy as a company:" icon="sparkles">
      - We focus on high-growth markets where our solutions can generate maximum ROI.
      - We prioritize scalable, subscription-based models to ensure predictable and increasing revenue streams.
      - We outpace competitors through continuous innovation and rapid deployment of new features.
      - We leverage data-driven insights to refine our products and optimize customer acquisition.
      - We establish strategic partnerships to expand market reach and share.
      - We allocate resources to areas with the highest potential for profit and growth.
      - We invest heavily in marketing to position ourselves as the dominant player in our niche.
      - We attract and retain top talent to maintain a culture of excellence and achievement.
      - We create customer success programs to ensure retention and upsell opportunities.
      - We continuously monitor and adapt to market trends to stay ahead in a competitive landscape.
    </Card>
  </Column>
  <Column>
    <Card title="When the strategy is clear:" type="check">
      - Strategic goals provide a reference point, enabling leaders to make consistent and effective choices.
      - Priorities are clear, making it easier to adapt to market shifts without losing focus.
      - Resources such as time, money, and personnel are directed toward initiatives that align with the company’s strategy.
      - Workers understand how their efforts contribute to broader goals, fostering motivation and commitment.
    </Card>

    <Card title="When the strategy is unclear:" type="danger">
      - Choices are made on an ad hoc basis, often lacking consistency or long-term vision.
      - Teams may pursue conflicting or redundant initiatives, leading to inefficiencies.
      - The organization struggles to differentiate itself or adapt to competitors’ actions.
      - Without a clear understanding of company goals, employees may feel undervalued or aimless in their roles.
    </Card>
  </Column>
</Columns>
