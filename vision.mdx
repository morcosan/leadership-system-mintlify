<Columns cols={2}>
  <Column>
    <Card title="Definition" type="note" icon="🤓">
      "Vision" = a clear, aspirational statement that defines what an organization aims to achieve in the long term. It describes the desired future state and serves as a north star for strategic decision-making and goal-setting.
    </Card>

    <Card title="Our vision as a company:">
      - We envision becoming the premier SaaS provider for businesses seeking exponential growth.
      - We see ourselves as leaders in the marketplace, shaping the future of profitable business solutions.
      - We aim to build a brand synonymous with success, innovation, and financial performance.
      - We strive to create a world where our tools are indispensable to businesses aiming to win.
      - We aspire to set the standard for excellence in profit-driven SaaS solutions.
      - We imagine a future where our dominance in the market is a reflection of our customers’ victories.
      - We envision scaling our impact and revenue to unprecedented heights.
      - We aim to unlock new opportunities for wealth creation for our clients and ourselves.
      - We see our company as a platform that turns ambition into market leadership.
      - We aspire to become the go-to name for businesses that want to succeed in capitalism’s competitive arena.
    </Card>
  </Column>
  <Column>
    <Card title="When the vision is clear:" type="check" icon="😇">
      - A clear vision defines what the company stands for, strengthening its identity internally and externally.
      - Decisions are aligned with long-term objectives, ensuring that efforts contribute to achieving the vision.
      - Employees are inspired by a compelling vision, driving them to contribute to the company’s long-term success.
      - A strong vision appeals to candidates who share similar aspirations, enhancing recruitment efforts.
    </Card>

    <Card title="When the vision is unclear:" type="danger" icon="😨">
      - A lack of vision makes it hard for the company to define its role and purpose, both internally and externally.
      - Decisions become reactive and short-sighted, causing the company to deviate from its potential trajectory.
      - Teams and departments may operate with conflicting goals, leading to inefficiencies and conflicts.
      - Employees may feel uninspired or aimless without a compelling picture of the future.
    </Card>
  </Column>
</Columns>
